# XephiraOS Official Website & Download Portal

Official source repository for [xephiraos.github.io](https://xephiraos.github.io).

## Design System
- **Engine**: Hydro-Glass Pure Water Optical Engine (`liquid-glass.css`)
- **Optics**: Surface tension curvature, fluid wave dynamics, specular rim sheen, and crystal-clear water translucency
- **Aesthetic**: Pure clean white default background (Light mode) and deep aquatic midnight (Dark mode) with zero colored neon glow
- **Typography**: Outfit, Inter, and JetBrains Mono

## Pages
- `index.html`: Official landing page featuring interactive 3D phone canvas with Pure Water OS, Settings Home, and About Phone previews, plus live interactive water ripple playground
- `downloads.html`: Official download portal with real-time search, OEM filter chips, device specs, GApps vs Vanilla flavor selector, direct ROM download links, and SHA-256 verification
