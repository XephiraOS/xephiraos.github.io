Rendering Innovation

The Kyant0 Liquid Glass Architecture

Unlike ordinary blur overlays, XephiraOS implements hardware-accelerated AGSL RuntimeShaders calculating analytical distance fields and surface normal refraction in real time.

📐

SDF Meniscus Geometry

Signed Distance Field (SDF) rounded rectangle algorithms determine the exact sub-pixel boundary curvature, creating physical liquid meniscus edges that bend light naturally.

✨

Specular Rim Lighting

Continuous dual-layer specular reflections react to angle of incidence, producing an ultra-clean, glistening glass edge without distracting rainbow chromatic fringing.

💧

Crystal Transparency

Pure monochrome optical dispersion keeps underlying content completely legible while preserving depth, tactile hierarchy, and 120 FPS render throughput.

Your Data Stays Yours

Algorithmic Battery Mastery

All-Day Endurance

  • Real-time cycle count and battery health metrics
