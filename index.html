System Pillars

Engineered For Perfection

XephiraOS is built from the ground up for power users who demand uncompromising performance, zero manufacturer telemetry, and surgical control.

⚡

Bare-Metal Performance

Zero Micro-Stutters

We eradicated bloatware, background diagnostic daemons, and tracking metrics. Optimized CPU governor scheduling ensures snappy app launches and continuous 120Hz refresh stability.

  • Clean AOSP / LineageOS 23.2 & 24.0 base
  • Optimized memory management & zRAM compression
  • Game Space with bypass charging & thermal limits
🛡️

Hardened Privacy & Security

Your Data Stays Yours

Experience granular hardware sensor kill switches, strict per-app network firewalls, and enforcing SELinux policies verified by automated daily test suites.

  • Built-in per-app internet & WiFi access toggle
  • Up-to-date monthly Android security patches
  • Camera, microphone, and clipboard access indicators
🔋

Algorithmic Battery Mastery

All-Day Endurance

No intrusive "AI battery killers" that close your background apps prematurely. We use kernel-level wake-lock throttling and adaptive sleep routines that let you sleep without draining battery.

  • Deep Doze mode with 0.4% per hour standby drain
  • Smart charging cut-off at 80% to preserve battery lifespan
  • Real-time cycle count and battery health metrics
🎨

Pure Liquid Customization

Tailored Elegance

Every element is customizable without sacrificing visual cohesion. Enjoy custom status bar icons, lock screen clocks, themed icon packs, and system-wide font selection.
